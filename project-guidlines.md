Build exercise: ship something small
Build and deploy a full-stack app on DeepSpace. Your idea, your call. A focused, finished project is stronger than a large, incomplete one.

Choose a scope you can finish well within the five-day submission window. We care about your judgment and explanation as much as feature count. If something remains unfinished, tell us what you would do next.

Build an interactive app with enough technical depth to demonstrate your understanding of DeepSpace. Use integrations where they improve the product or user experience; there is no minimum integration count. Explain your choices and tradeoffs, including why you left out features or integrations that would not add value.

A simple app such as a basic habit tracker is usually not enough unless it adds something meaningfully new. StoryNest, ThreadHunt, and RunningMap are examples of stronger projects. Use them to understand the bar, then make the idea your own.
